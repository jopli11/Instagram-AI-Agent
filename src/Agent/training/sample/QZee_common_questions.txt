## Features & Functionality

### Can clients book appointments outside of business hours?
Yes! One of the biggest advantages of QZee is that your booking page works 24/7, allowing clients to schedule appointments whenever it's convenient for them. Our data shows that about 35% of bookings happen outside regular business hours, which means you're capturing business even while you sleep. You maintain full control over which time slots are available, and the system automatically prevents bookings during times you've marked as unavailable.

### How does QZee help reduce no-shows?
QZee tackles no-shows through several effective strategies. Our automated reminder system sends customizable SMS and email notifications at intervals you define (like 24 hours and 1 hour before appointments). You can also require deposits or full payments at booking, which significantly increases commitment. For clients who do miss appointments, our system can automatically tag them for follow-up or apply your cancellation policy. These features typically reduce no-shows by 25-30%.

### Can I customize the booking page to match my brand?
Absolutely! QZee offers complete white-label customization. You can add your logo, select your brand colors, use custom imagery, and even set up a branded URL (like booking.yourbusiness.com). The booking page is fully responsive and looks great on any device. You can also customize the booking flow, add intake forms, and create a seamless experience that feels like an extension of your website.

### Does QZee integrate with my existing tools?
QZee was built with integration in mind. We connect seamlessly with popular calendar systems (Google, Outlook, Apple), payment processors (Stripe, PayPal, Square), marketing tools (Mailchimp, HubSpot), business software (QuickBooks, Xero, Salesforce), and communication platforms (Twilio, SendGrid). If you use a specialized tool that's not on our standard integration list, our API makes custom integrations possible too.

### Can I manage multiple staff members and locations?
Yes, QZee excels at managing complex scheduling scenarios. You can set individual availability for each staff member, assign services based on skills and qualifications, and manage multiple locations from a single dashboard. Clients can book with specific staff members or let the system suggest the best match based on availability and expertise. You can also set up resource management for rooms, equipment, or other assets that need to be scheduled alongside staff.

## Technical Questions

### Is QZee cloud-based or do I need to install software?
QZee is a fully cloud-based SaaS (Software as a Service) solution, which means there's nothing to install or maintain on your end. You access the system through any web browser, and your clients book through your customized booking page. We also offer mobile apps for both you and your clients to manage appointments on the go. All updates and maintenance are handled automatically, so you're always using the latest version.

### How does QZee handle different time zones?
QZee has sophisticated time zone management built in. The system automatically detects your clients' time zones and displays available slots in their local time, while maintaining your schedule in your time zone. This eliminates confusion and prevents scheduling errors when working with clients in different geographical areas. All confirmations and reminders also include clear time zone information to ensure everyone is on the same page.

### What happens if there's an internet outage?
While QZee requires internet access for full functionality, we've built in several safeguards for connectivity issues. The system automatically saves your work as you go, so you won't lose data if your connection drops. Our mobile app can also function with limited capabilities during brief outages. For planned internet maintenance, you can download your day's schedule in advance. Once connectivity is restored, the system automatically syncs any updates.

### Can QZee handle high traffic volumes?
Absolutely. Our infrastructure is built on enterprise-grade cloud services with automatic scaling to handle traffic spikes. Whether you're running a flash sale, launching a new service, or experiencing seasonal demand, QZee maintains fast performance even with thousands of simultaneous users. We regularly stress-test the system to ensure reliability during peak periods.

### How often is the system updated?
We release updates on a regular schedule, typically every 2-3 weeks for minor improvements and quarterly for major feature additions. Updates happen seamlessly in the background without disrupting your service. For significant changes, we provide advance notice and documentation to help you take advantage of new capabilities. We also maintain a public roadmap so you can see what enhancements are coming and provide input on future development.
