# QZee Smart Booking Solution: Detailed Features & Benefits

## Core Platform Features

### Smart Scheduling Engine
- **AI-Powered Optimization**: Automatically suggests optimal time slots based on staff availability, customer preferences, and historical booking patterns
- **Dynamic Availability**: Real-time updates to availability as bookings are made or canceled
- **Buffer Time Management**: Automatically adds preparation or cleanup time between appointments
- **Recurring Appointment Support**: Easily set up regular appointments with customizable frequency
- **Group Booking Capabilities**: Schedule multiple clients for the same service simultaneously (classes, workshops, etc.)

### Customizable Booking Pages
- **White-Label Solution**: Fully brandable booking pages that match your business identity
- **Custom URL Options**: Create personalized booking links for your business or individual staff members
- **Responsive Design**: Perfect viewing experience across all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Multi-Language Support**: Offer booking pages in multiple languages to serve diverse clientele
- **Custom Fields**: Add intake forms, questionnaires, or special requests to the booking process

### Client Management
- **Comprehensive Client Profiles**: Store contact information, booking history, preferences, and notes
- **Smart Segmentation**: Group clients based on booking frequency, service preferences, or custom criteria
- **Communication History**: Track all client interactions in one centralized location
- **Client Self-Service Portal**: Allow clients to manage their own appointments, update information, and view history
- **Digital Intake Forms**: Collect necessary information before appointments

### Staff & Resource Management
- **Individual Staff Calendars**: Manage availability for each team member separately
- **Skill-Based Assignment**: Automatically route bookings to staff with appropriate skills
- **Resource Allocation**: Manage rooms, equipment, or other resources alongside staff scheduling
- **Performance Analytics**: Track staff utilization, booking rates, and client satisfaction
- **Mobile Staff App**: Allow staff to view and manage their schedules on the go

### Automated Communications
- **Appointment Reminders**: Reduce no-shows with customizable SMS and email reminders
- **Booking Confirmations**: Automatic confirmation messages when appointments are scheduled
- **Follow-Up Messages**: Post-appointment communications to gather feedback or encourage rebooking
- **Waitlist Notifications**: Alert clients when previously unavailable slots open up
- **Custom Marketing Messages**: Send targeted promotions based on booking history and preferences

### Payment Processing
- **Secure Payment Gateway**: Accept credit cards, digital wallets, and other payment methods through our Stripe integration
- **Affordable Pricing**: Just £15 per month with no hidden costs or additional fees
- **Deposit Requirements**: Require full or partial payment at booking to reduce no-shows
- **Cancellation Policies**: Automatically enforce your business's cancellation and refund rules
- **Package & Membership Billing**: Sell and track service packages or recurring memberships
- **Invoicing System**: Generate and send professional invoices for services rendered

### Business Intelligence
- **Comprehensive Dashboard**: Visual overview of key business metrics
- **Booking Analytics**: Track booking patterns, popular services, peak times, and more
- **Revenue Reports**: Analyze income by service type, staff member, or time period
- **Client Insights**: Understand client demographics, retention rates, and lifetime value
- **Customizable Reports**: Create and save custom reports for your specific business needs
